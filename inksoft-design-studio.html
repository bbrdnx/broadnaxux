InkSoft

Design Studio: Signs & Banners

A new revenue module for print shop owners, built from scratch in under three months.

About InkSoft

InkSoft is a SaaS platform for print shops, apparel decorators, and promotional product vendors. Subscribers run custom-branded microstores, manage orders, and streamline production from a single workflow.

Role

Product Designer

Timeline

Under 3 months to MVP

Outcome

New revenue module shipped on time

The Signs & Banners Design Studio. Live previews and real-time pricing on products nobody else online lets you customize.

Context

A product customers were already asking for

InkSoft had just relaunched their Design Studio, the online tool that lets consumers customize hundreds of products and send them straight to a print shop. It gave mom-and-pop shops the e-commerce capabilities of giants like Custom Ink, and the new version was faster, more intuitive, and finally mobile-friendly.

Almost immediately, shop owners asked for the next piece: a way to design and order signs and banners. The market gap was wide open. Plenty of printers offered customizable signs (size, paper type, finish), but none of them let you preview the design and see the price live.

The Opportunity

A client-funded sprint with long-term upside

Six months after the relaunch, a former licensee came back. They'd left when InkSoft shifted focus away from apparel, but the new Studio drew them in with a proposition: build us a Signs & Banners module, and we'll fund the whole thing.

  • Full development cost covered.
  • Three-year enterprise license at signing.
  • InkSoft was already planning to build this (just not yet).
  • InkSoft could license the module to other clients with no attribution.

The catch: MVP by the first week of January. Less than three months away. (For context, the original Design Studio took two years.)

Process

Moving fast without losing the thread

Both product teams renegotiated their Q4 goals so we could take this on. We broke the work into two epics and ran them in parallel. Engineering started immediately on the API (enhanced zoom, vector canvas for infinitely scalable designs) while QA wrote their plan. I went straight to the research gold mine: Support.

The timeline forced clarity. Decisions made over Slack, finalized at standup. Every design move defensible and buildable within days, not weeks.

Outcome

Shipped on time, built to scale

We shipped the MVP on schedule. The module gave InkSoft customers something no competitor offered: live customization with real-time pricing on signs and banners. It paid for itself three ways over, through the original investor's enterprise license, the new licensees who came in after, and the stickiness it added across the customer base.
