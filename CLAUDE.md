# Barbara Broadnax — Portfolio Site Guidelines

## Voice & Writing Rules
- **No em-dashes.** Use a comma, period, or rewrite the sentence instead.
- Keep copy concise and direct.
- Descriptions on cards should be one sentence max, active voice.
